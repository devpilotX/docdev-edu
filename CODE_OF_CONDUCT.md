# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project is a working environment. Everyone participating in it is expected
to behave the way a good colleague behaves: be direct about the work, be decent
about the people.

**Expected**

- Give specific, actionable feedback on the change, not the person.
- Assume competence and good faith; ask before assuming a mistake.
- Accept a reasoned disagreement and move on once a decision is made.

**Unacceptable**

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or review.

## Scope

This applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any
communication that represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to `conduct@docdev.edu`. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently remove a participant,
in proportion to the behaviour and its recurrence.

This code of conduct is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant,
version 2.1.
